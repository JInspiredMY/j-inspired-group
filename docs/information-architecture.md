# Information Architecture — journeyinspired.group

8 pages. No more, no fewer, without a documented decision to change scope.

```
Home
├── Our Story
├── Ecosystem Model
├── Partnership Opportunities
├── Governance
├── Press
├── Careers
└── Contact
```

Flat structure — no sub-navigation, no nested routes beyond this. `.group` does not need deep IA; it's a credibility site, not a content hub.

Note: `.group` is not the SEO play. Target-keyword fields below are mostly N/A — organic discovery is journeycurated.com's job. Where a `.group` page could plausibly rank (e.g., "Journey Inspired Group," "[Company] investor relations"), it's noted, but do not optimize `.group` copy for search volume at the expense of institutional-audience clarity.

---

## 1. Home

- **Domain:** journeyinspired.group
- **URL Slug:** `/`
- **Primary Audience:** All institutional visitors (first touch)
- **Primary Goal:** Authority + orientation → route to Partnership Opportunities or Contact
- **Target Keyword:** "Journey Inspired Group" (brand only, not competitive)
- **Page Sections:**
  1. Hero — brand promise ("Curating Transformational Travel Experiences"), NOT the AI platform-positioning line
  2. Group overview — one paragraph
  3. 5 pillars — equal-weight cards, names only, no sub-category detail
  4. One line acknowledging Curated Experiences as "how we design every journey" — not a card, not a section
  5. Partnership CTA (primary)
  6. Contact CTA (secondary)
- **Primary CTA:** → Partnership Opportunities
- **Secondary CTA:** → Contact
- **Internal Links To:** Ecosystem Model (pillar cards), Partnership Opportunities, Contact
- **Internal Links From:** every other page (nav)
- **SEO Meta Title:** Journey Inspired Group | Curating Transformational Travel Experiences
- **SEO Meta Description:** Corporate overview — company positioning, not a keyword-target description.
