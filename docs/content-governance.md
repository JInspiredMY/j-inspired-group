# Content Governance — journeyinspired.group

Single reference for every copy/content decision on this domain. If a copywriting prompt or Copilot suggestion conflicts with this file, this file wins.

## Canonical pillar taxonomy

Use these exact names, this exact order, everywhere:

1. **Health & Wellness Tourism** — medical travel facilitation, preventive health, wellness retreats, longevity, men's health, mental wellbeing
2. **Education & Experiential Learning Tourism** — study tours, university visits, professional/corporate learning journeys, skills immersion, educational exchanges
3. **Property & Lifestyle Tourism** — property discovery, MM2H, investment familiarisation, retirement lifestyle, relocation journeys, luxury lifestyle
4. **Nature, Eco & Adventure Tourism** — national parks, marine tourism, hiking, conservation, community tourism, sustainable travel
5. **Culture & Heritage Tourism** — culinary, heritage, arts, festivals, indigenous experiences, local living, creative workshops

**Do not use any other pillar naming found elsewhere** — an earlier internal governance reference has a stale 4-name version of this list with different naming ("Eco Tourism," "Property & Lifestyle Discovery," "Group Travel" listed as a pillar). This document supersedes it.

Not pillars, do not surface as top-level categories:
- **Curated Experiences** — a methodology/tag applied across all 5 pillars. One sentence on the Ecosystem Model page. Not a page, not a nav item, not a card.
- **Corporate Travel Experiences / Group Travel** — product delivery formats, can apply within any pillar. Not top-level categories.

## AI positioning — the rule that matters most

| Line | Status | Where it can appear |
|---|---|---|
| "Curating Transformational Travel Experiences" | Brand promise. Usable now. | Anywhere, including hero. |
| "AI-Powered Health, Wellness, Education & Property Travel Concierge" | Future platform positioning. **Not a current capability claim.** | Only in clearly future/vision-framed content (e.g., Our Story's "where we're headed" section). **Never** as present-tense homepage or hero copy. |

Phase 1 is confirmed non-AI. Any copy that implies AI is a current, live capability of the platform is a factual overstatement — treat it as a compliance issue, not a style preference.

## Content types excluded from this domain

- **Articles, blog posts, destination guides, thought leadership** — not part of the current 8-page institutional build. journeycurated.com's content now consolidates in-domain at `/journey-curated` per the single-domain decision, but its page-by-page IA/CMS/tagging scope is **not yet decided** — do not build it under this reconciliation. See `docs/journeyinspired-group_design-brief_v1.4.md` Appendix A.
- **Product/package listings, pricing, booking flows** — belongs to `.travel`.
- **Job listings / applicant tracking** — Careers is interest-form only until real openings exist.

## Gated sections — placeholder rules

These sections ship as minimal, honest placeholders, not fabricated content, until their gating condition is met:

| Section | Gate | Do not do |
|---|---|---|
| Investor Relations content | Monetization + traction milestones (not a calendar date) | Do not publish financial projections, growth claims, or investor materials before this is real |
| Careers — job listings | Real open positions | Do not fabricate roles or build a full careers portal |
| Governance — leadership/board | Real leadership assets confirmed | Do not fabricate bios, titles, or board composition |
| Governance — legal entity details | Confirmed legal entity information | Do not fabricate registration numbers, structure, or filings |

## Compliance flags (not resolved by this document — route to legal/compliance review)

- **MM2H (Malaysia My Second Home) references** — regulatory program, naming and claims need accuracy review before publishing under Property & Lifestyle Tourism.
- **Medical travel / medical facilitation language** — healthcare-adjacent claims need review before publishing under Health & Wellness Tourism, particularly anything implying medical advice, outcomes, or facility endorsements.

## Tone

Institutional, not consumer. Calm, confident, evidence-based. No marketing clichés, no urgency language, no "book now" patterns anywhere on this domain.
